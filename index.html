01 — About us

About BellMitchell

BellMitchell is a small web studio that builds websites for businesses: the cafés, clinics, trades and shops that keep a town running. We make sites that look warm, load fast and bring in customers, then we stick around to keep them that way.

  • Made to be enjoyed

    Design with personality, so visitors remember you, not a template.

  • Built to perform

    Fast pages, clear calls to action and search basics done right from day one.

  • People, not tickets

    One small team from first call to launch, and a real person when you need help after.
